# Research agenda

## Central question
**What recommendation utility is retained when personalization is restricted to explicit opt-in preferences and low-support items are suppressed?**

## Hypotheses
### H1
A minimum cohort-support threshold reduces exposure of rare signals at a measurable relevance cost.

### H2
Explicit-preference-only scoring remains useful in domains with strong content features.

### H3
Empty-preference behavior can avoid hidden inference without producing fabricated personalization.

## Experiments
1. Generate item catalogs with varying feature frequency and measure relevance vs support threshold.
2. Vary number of opt-in preferences and compare ranking stability.
3. Add a future formal privacy baseline and clearly separate its guarantees from this heuristic design.

## Metrics
- top-k relevance on synthetic preferences
- catalog coverage
- suppression rate
- ranking stability
- rare-feature exposure
